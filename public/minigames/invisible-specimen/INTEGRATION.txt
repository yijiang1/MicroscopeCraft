The Invisible Specimen — version 2: Across Scales

Open index.html directly, or run npm run dev from the repository root and visit
/minigames/invisible-specimen/index.html on the local Vite server.
One self-contained HTML file; no dependencies or required network requests.

Ten assignments:
1. Transparent swimmer (60 μm), phase contrast.
2. Pigmented alga (18 μm), bright field.
3. Salt microcrystal (120 μm), low-magnification shape identification.
4. Glass fiber (200 μm), dark-field edges.
5. Rod bacterium (3 μm), high-NA optical imaging.
6. Diatom shell (30 μm) with 270 nm detail; blue versus red light.
7. Metal nanoprism (60 nm), prepared electron grid.
8. Prepared virus-like particle (120 nm), electron imaging.
9. Crystal lattice patch (4 nm) with a 220 pm periodic feature.
10. Defective lattice patch (5 nm) with a missing image feature.

Controls: pointer / arrow keys aim; 1–3 optical contrast mode; [ ] focus;
Click a specimen to lock it as the zoom target (dashed outline). Magnification
changes center on its current world position, independently of pointer movement
to the dropdown. Without an explicit lock, the last hovered specimen is retained.
With no candidate, zoom preserves the view center. Click empty space to release
the lock. C centers the selected specimen, or the crosshair if no specimen is known. Find slide resets stage and overview magnification.
Space / photograph captures. Assignments can be selected in any order.

Optical magnifications: 100×, 400×, 1000×, 2000× digital enlargement.
Electron magnifications: 10,000× to 4,000,000×.
Illumination: white (550 nm reference), red 650 nm, blue 450 nm,
TEM 100 kV (3.70 pm wavelength), HRTEM 200 kV (2.51 pm wavelength).

Model:
Lengths in μm. FOV diameter = 80,000 / magnification (μm).
Optical resolution = 0.61 × wavelength / NA. NA is 0.25, 0.65, or 1.2,
with NA unchanged for the 2000× digital enlargement.
Electron resolution is a separate illustrative instrument limit:
100 kV preset: 0.35 nm; 200 kV HRTEM preset: 0.18 nm.
The preset difference represents different instrument capabilities, not a
universal rule that higher accelerating voltage always resolves smaller detail.
Rendering, blur and contrast are qualitative, not a wave-optical simulation.
Live/optical slides cannot be sent to the electron instrument. Electron missions
load a prepared, static sample; optical contrast controls are disabled there.
Decoy sizes vary. Larger specimens require lower magnification to frame fully.

Progress:
Journal photos/settings saved under localStorage key invisible-specimen-v2.
Storage errors fall back to session-only operation. Reset journal asks for
confirmation. Version 1 progress is not migrated. Reload begins on assignment 1;
recorded entries remain marked and may be replayed.

MicroscopeCraft integration:
The game is located at public/minigames/invisible-specimen/index.html. Show it
in a same-origin iframe. Pause world movement and unlock its pointer controls.
After the iframe loads, listen on iframe.contentWindow for CustomEvent:
  invisible-specimen:complete
Detail:
{ game: 'invisible-specimen', version: 2, completed: true,
  reward: 'across-scales', specimens: [ten specimen IDs], shots: number }

Completion fires after a capture fills all ten journal entries, once per page
session unless the journal is reset. Grant host rewards idempotently.
The standalone game does not mutate MicroscopeCraft inventory.
API: iframe.contentWindow.invisibleSpecimen.getState() and .restart().
restart() restarts the lab on assignment 1 and preserves the saved journal.
Remove the iframe to release its animation, listeners and observer.

Verification:
JavaScript syntax; all ten assignments solvable at desktop and small-screen
sizes using suitable magnifications; resolution, optical wavelength, overzoom,
and live-slide preparation gates. Browser-tested salt and atomic-lattice
capture, centering/zoom, saved progress after reload, and red/blue resolution
feedback. Original version's movement, pause and optical mode playthrough was
also tested before expansion.

Campus reward bridge (integrated):
The Science Building activity console embeds the game. Each successful capture
sends a same-origin parent message:
  { type: 'microscopecraft:specimen', specimen: '<mission type>' }
The host checks the origin, active iframe source and ten supported mission IDs.
It records invisible_<mission type> with score 1. Saved reward milestones prevent
duplicate coins/vouchers when replaying or resetting the standalone journal.
Closing the campus activity removes the iframe; its journal remains saved.
